Affiliate disclosure

True Car Cost UK is free to use. To help cover the cost of running it, some links on the site may be affiliate links.

What that means

If you click an affiliate link and then buy something or take out a policy, the company may pay us a small commission. You pay exactly the same price as you would going direct. Affiliate links may come through networks such as Awin.

How we label them

Any affiliate link will be marked near the link itself, for example with "(affiliate link)" or a short note at the top of the section. Links to GOV.UK, MoneyHelper and similar free public services are never affiliate links.

What it does not change

  • The calculator's maths is the same whatever car, lender or insurer you are looking at.
  • No company can pay to change a guide's advice or a result.
  • We only link to products and services that we think are useful for keeping car costs down, such as comparing insurance quotes.

The site is not a lender, credit broker or insurance broker and does not give regulated financial advice. Always read the terms of any finance or insurance product before you sign up.
