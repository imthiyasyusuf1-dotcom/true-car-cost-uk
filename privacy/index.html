Privacy policy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ads tracking you, and the numbers you type never leave your device.

What you type into the calculator

All calculations run in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us or anyone else. If you press "Copy link to this setup", your numbers are put into the web address so you can share them. Only people you send that link to will see them.

Visit counting

We count visits and clicks on outbound links using a simple counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). It records a number going up by one. No cookies are set and no personal profile is built. To count each browser only once a day, the site saves today's date in your browser's local storage. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are passed through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and delivered to our private inbox. We only use them to reply to you and delete them when they are no longer needed. See the FormSubmit privacy policy.

Links to other sites

We link to GOV.UK and other useful sites. Some links may in future be affiliate links (see our affiliate disclosure). When you click through, that site's own privacy policy and cookies apply, and an affiliate network may set a cookie so the site knows you came from us.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you unless you send us a message, so there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question, contact us. You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
