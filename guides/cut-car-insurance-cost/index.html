How to cut your car insurance cost

Updated 18 October 2026

Insurance is often the second biggest car cost, and the one you can change fastest. These are the things that tend to make a real difference.

  1. Buy about three weeks before renewal. Quotes tend to be cheapest when you buy around 20 to 26 days ahead. Leaving it to the day before usually costs more.
  2. Compare more than one site. Comparison sites do not all list the same insurers, and some insurers are not on any comparison site at all, so check a couple of direct quotes too.
  3. Pay annually if you can. Paying monthly is usually a loan with interest added. The APR on monthly car insurance is often well into double figures.
  4. Be accurate about mileage. Do not guess high. Check your last two MOT certificates on GOV.UK to see what you really drive.
  5. Check your job title. Some job titles are priced differently even when they mean the same thing. Only ever pick a title that honestly describes what you do.
  6. Think about the voluntary excess. A higher excess lowers the premium, but only set it at a level you could actually pay after an accident.
  7. Add an experienced named driver if they really do drive the car sometimes. Never put a parent down as the main driver of a car you mainly drive. That is fronting, and it is fraud.
  8. Look at a black box policy if you are under 25. Good driving can bring the price down at renewal.
  9. Drop extras you already have. Breakdown cover, legal cover and key cover are often duplicated through a bank account or a separate policy.

Renewal prices

Since January 2022 insurers have not been allowed to charge a renewing customer more than they would charge a new customer for the same cover. That does not mean your renewal is the best price on the market, so it still pays to shop around every year.

Before you buy the car

Get an insurance quote for the exact car before you buy it. Two cars with the same price can sit in very different insurance groups. Put the quote into the calculator alongside the finance so you see the full monthly cost, not just the payment.
