About True Car Cost UK

True Car Cost UK is a free calculator that shows what a car really costs you each month, not just the finance payment on the advert.

Why it exists

Car adverts lead with one number: the monthly payment. But fuel, insurance, road tax, servicing, MOT, tyres, parking and the money the car loses in value often add up to more than the payment itself. People find this out after they have signed. This site puts all of it in one place so you can see the full monthly figure, the cost per mile and the share of your take home pay before you commit.

Who runs it

It is an independent site built and run by one independent developer in the UK. It is not owned by a lender, dealer, insurer or comparison site.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Finance payments use the standard loan formula from the APR, term, deposit and (for PCP) the balloon payment.
  • Fuel is miles divided by mpg, converted to litres (4.546 litres to a UK gallon), times the price per litre.
  • Take home pay is a rough estimate using England income tax, National Insurance and student loan thresholds, with no pension.
  • For cash buyers the yearly loss in value is counted as a cost, because it is one.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The figures are estimates to help you decide, not financial advice.

How the site is paid for

The calculator and guides are free and always will be. In the future some links may be affiliate links, which means the site could earn a small commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. Any such links will be labelled. See the affiliate disclosure.
