    Quick answers

    How much of my pay should a car take?

    A common rule of thumb is keeping all car costs under about 15% of take home pay. Under 10% is comfortable. Over 20% and the car starts running your life.

    PCP or HP?

    PCP gives lower monthly payments because you're only paying off the drop in value, but you don't own the car unless you pay the balloon. HP costs more a month but the car is yours at the end. If you plan to keep the car for years, HP usually works out cheaper overall.

    Why count depreciation if I paid cash?

    Because it's real money. A £16k car that's worth £10k after three years cost you £6k just by sitting on your drive. Ignoring it makes cash buying look cheaper than it is.

    Is my data saved anywhere?

    No. Everything runs in your browser. The share link just stores the numbers in the address so you can send it to someone.
